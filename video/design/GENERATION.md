# 배경 생성 기록

방식: Codex 내장 image_gen. 사용자 API 키 사용 없음. 각 배경은 불투명 PNG 원본이며 글자와 인물이 없습니다.

## 금융 스튜디오
Use case: illustration-story. Create a vertical 9:16 empty background plate for a Korean finance explainer motion comic. A tasteful modern financial news studio in precise hand-drawn Korean webtoon style: thin charcoal ink contours, subtle hatching and mostly soft flat shading, NOT photorealism or glossy 3D. Ivory walls, muted blue-gray panels, restrained deep navy accents, one subtle teal LED detail, soft diffuse daylight. Frontal eye-level medium view, no extreme perspective. Quiet large blank presentation panel across upper-middle left, subtle architectural details near outer edges, lower half uncluttered with no desk or foreground objects so waist-up character overlays will read clearly. Upper 15 percent and lower 20 percent visually quiet for later titles and subtitles. No people, silhouettes, lettering, numbers, logos, tickers, stock charts, watermarks or decorative text. Sophisticated calm editorial backdrop matching black business suit characters with warm natural skin. Portrait high-resolution, full-bleed opaque background.

## 사무실
Create a portrait 9:16 background plate for a Korean office finance motion comic. Empty contemporary Korean financial services office meeting corner, clean precise Korean webtoon ink linework, subtle crosshatch and softly colored illustration rendering, same restrained palette as a financial news studio: ivory, warm light gray, muted blue gray, charcoal, small teal accents. Eye-level frontal view, a large blank pale wall/presentation board upper center, frosted glass partitions near edges, a narrow window on one edge with soft city daylight, small out-of-focus shelf or plant only far side edge. Spacious calm interior, no people, no foreground table or chairs obstructing lower center. Central and lower area kept quiet so TWO waist-up character sprites can be layered side by side. Top 15 percent and bottom 20 percent visually quiet for later titles and subtitle cards. No words, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no charts, no watermark. Detailed enough to establish an office but low contrast enough not to fight black suit character ink contours. Flat illustrative color, not photorealistic, not glossy 3D. Single opaque full bleed background.
